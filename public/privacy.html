Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information Maska Sub collects and how it's used.

1. What we collect

When you create an account or place an order, we collect: your phone number, a securely hashed version of your password (we never store your actual password), your email address (for payment receipts), and your order history.

2. How your password is protected

Your password is never stored as plain text. It is scrambled using a one-way hashing method (bcrypt) before being saved, meaning even we cannot see or recover your actual password.

3. Payment information

We never see or store your card details. All payments are processed directly by Paystack, a licensed payment processor.

4. How we use your information

Your phone number and network selection are used solely to deliver the data or airtime you purchase. Your email is used only for payment receipts and, if needed, to contact you about an order issue.

5. Who we share information with

We share only what's necessary with our payment processor (Paystack) and our data delivery provider (VTpass) to complete your order. We do not sell or share your information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

6. Data retention

We keep your order history so you can review past purchases and so we can help resolve any delivery issues. You may request account deletion by contacting support.

7. Your rights

You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, or request that your account and associated data be deleted, by contacting support via WhatsApp.

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical safeguards — including rate-limiting, encrypted password storage, and secure connections — to protect your information. No system is 100% immune to risk, but we take this seriously.

This is a general privacy policy template, written to be honest about current practices, and is not a substitute for formal legal advice under Nigeria's Data Protection Act (NDPA).
